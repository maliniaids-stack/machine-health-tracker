# 🎥 3-Minute Demo Video Script

This is a super simple, step-by-step guide for your 3-minute demonstration video. It tells you exactly what to show on your screen and what to say.

---

## Part 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
**🎬 What to show on screen:** 
Open your VS Code and show the `README.md` file so people can see the project title.

**🗣️ What to say:**
> "Hello everyone! This is my Machine Condition Monitor project. Imagine a big factory machine, like a giant motor. If it gets too hot or starts shaking too violently, it might break and cost a lot of money. My project acts like a smart doctor for the machine. It watches the machine and warns us *before* it breaks!"

---

## Part 2: Starting the Brain (0:30 - 1:15)
**🎬 What to show on screen:** 
1. Open your terminal in VS Code.
2. Type `npm start` to show the backend turning on.
3. Show your second terminal where the `localtunnel` is running.

**🗣️ What to say:**
> "First, we need to turn on the brain of the project. I will start my local server here in the terminal. This server is connected to a database that will save all of our data. Then, I open a magic internet tunnel so that our hardware can talk to my computer safely."

---

## Part 3: Turning on the Hardware (1:15 - 2:00)
**🎬 What to show on screen:** 
1. Switch your screen to the web browser showing the **Wokwi ESP32 Simulator**.
2. Click the big green **Play** button.
3. Highlight the black terminal box at the bottom of Wokwi where it says `HTTP Response code: 201`.

**🗣️ What to say:**
> "Next, let's turn on our fake factory machine! This little black board is an ESP32 micro-computer. I wrote C++ code that uses math to pretend it is a real machine vibrating and heating up. When I click play, it connects to Wi-Fi, filters out noise, and sends the data straight to our brain. See that '201' message? That means the data arrived successfully!"

---

## Part 4: The Dashboard (2:00 - 3:00)
**🎬 What to show on screen:** 
1. Switch to your web browser showing the `index.html` Dashboard.
2. Point your mouse at the "Machine Status" card.
3. Point your mouse at the lines on the charts as they move and update live.

**🗣️ What to say:**
> "Finally, let's look at the beautiful dashboard! As you can see, the data from our fake machine is appearing here completely live. The charts show the temperature and the vibration severity. Right now, it says 'Normal'. But if our machine gets too hot or shakes too hard, the dashboard will turn red to warn the factory workers. This is exactly how we can save machines from breaking down!"
